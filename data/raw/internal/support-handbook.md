# Customer Support Handbook (INTERNAL)

**Owner:** Customer Operations
**Version:** 4.2, last reviewed August 18, 2026
**Audience:** All support agents. Do not share with customers.

## 1. Service levels

| Channel | First response target | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Live chat | 2 minutes | Mon-Fri 08:00-20:00 CET and 08:00-20:00 ET |
| Email | 8 business hours | Mon-Fri |
| Phone (UK and US only) | 5 minutes | Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00 local |
| Reseller (B2B) portal | 4 business hours | Mon-Fri |

Tickets tagged `priority:high` (safety concerns, overheating, battery swelling) must be answered within **1 hour** on any channel, including weekends, by the on-call agent.

## 2. Refund and goodwill approval matrix

Agents may approve refunds and goodwill credits within their limit. Anything above the limit is escalated in the ticket using the **Request approval** macro.

| Amount (per order, in USD or equivalent) | Who can approve |
|---|---|
| Up to $150 | Any support agent |
| $150.01 to $500 | Senior Support Specialist |
| Above $500 | **Customer Operations Manager** |
| Any amount for a B2B/reseller order | Channel Sales Lead |

Team Leads coordinate shifts and quality reviews but **do not** have refund approval rights above the agent limit.

Goodwill credits (store credit given without a return) are capped at **$40 per customer per 12 months** regardless of approver, unless the Customer Operations Manager grants an exception.

## 3. Safety escalations

If a customer reports heat, smoke, a swollen battery or a burning smell:

1. Tell the customer to stop using and stop charging the product immediately, and to keep it away from flammable materials.
2. Tag the ticket `priority:high` and `safety`.
3. Do **not** ask the customer to open the product or to ship it with a standard label. Batteries with suspected damage need a special hazardous-goods pickup, arranged by the Product Safety team.
4. Offer a replacement or full refund regardless of the warranty status.

## 4. Tone and wording

- Use the customer's name, keep answers short, and give one clear next step.
- Never promise a delivery date for a replacement before the RMA is created.
- Do not speculate about unreleased products or firmware release dates.

## 5. Common macros

| Macro | Use |
|---|---|
| `RMA-start` | Opens a warranty replacement (see RMA process) |
| `Return-help` | Explains how to start a return in the account |
| `FW-update` | Step-by-step firmware update instructions |
| `Request approval` | Escalates a refund above the agent limit |
