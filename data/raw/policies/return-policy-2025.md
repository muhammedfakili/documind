# Lumora Audio Return Policy

**Effective date:** January 15, 2025
**Applies to:** Purchases made on lumora-audio.example and through the Lumora mobile app

We want you to love what you hear. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you can return it under the conditions below.

## Return window

- **All products, opened or unopened:** 30 days from the delivery date.
- Refurbished ("Lumora Certified Renewed") products: 15 days from the delivery date.

## Condition requirements

- Products must be returned with all original accessories (charging case, cables, ear tips, documentation).
- Opened products must be free of physical damage. Cosmetic signs of normal use are accepted.
- Products must be reset to factory settings and unpaired from the Lumora app account before shipping.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in at lumora-audio.example/account and open **Orders**.
2. Select the item and choose **Return item**.
3. Print the prepaid return label. Returns within the United States, the United Kingdom and the European Union are free of charge.
4. Drop the package at any carrier location within 7 days of creating the return.

## Refunds

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5 business days** after the returned item is inspected at our warehouse.
- Original shipping fees are refunded only if the return is due to a defect or a shipping error on our side.
- Gift purchases are refunded as Lumora store credit.

## Exceptions

- Personalized products (custom engraving) cannot be returned unless defective.
- Gift cards are non-refundable.

Questions? Contact support@lumora-audio.example.
