# Shipping Policy

**Last updated:** March 3, 2026

## Where we ship

We ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, all European Union member states, Norway, Switzerland, Australia and New Zealand. We do not currently ship to other countries. Orders with a shipping address outside these regions are cancelled automatically and refunded in full.

## Shipping options and costs

| Region | Standard | Express | Free standard shipping from |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States | 3-5 business days, $5.99 | 1-2 business days, $14.99 | $75 |
| Canada | 5-8 business days, CA$9.99 | 2-3 business days, CA$24.99 | CA$99 |
| United Kingdom | 2-4 business days, £4.99 | Next business day, £9.99 | £60 |
| European Union, Norway, Switzerland | 3-6 business days, €5.99 | 1-3 business days, €14.99 | €70 |
| Australia, New Zealand | 7-12 business days, A$14.99 | Not available | A$150 |

Orders placed before 2:00 PM local warehouse time on a business day ship the same day. Our warehouses are in Reno (USA), Leeds (UK) and Rotterdam (Netherlands).

## Duties and taxes

- Prices shown for the US and Canada exclude sales tax, which is added at checkout.
- Prices shown for the UK, EU, Norway and Switzerland include VAT. No import duties are charged on delivery.
- Orders to Australia and New Zealand may be subject to local import charges, paid by the recipient.

## Lost or damaged packages

If tracking has not updated for 7 business days, or the package arrives damaged, contact support within 30 days of the expected delivery date. We will send a replacement or issue a refund at no cost.

## Order changes

An order can be changed or cancelled until it is handed over to the carrier, usually within 2 hours of purchase. After that, use the return process.
