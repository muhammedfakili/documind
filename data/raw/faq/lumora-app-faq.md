# Lumora App — Help Center

The Lumora app (iOS and Android) lets you customize the sound, update firmware and find lost devices.

## Firmware updates

Firmware updates are installed through the app. Keep the earbuds in the open charging case, or keep the speaker or headphones connected to power, during the update. An update takes between 4 and 10 minutes. Do not close the app while the progress bar is visible.

If an update fails, the device automatically returns to the previous firmware version. Wait 5 minutes and try again. After three failed attempts, contact support with the error message shown in the app.

## Custom EQ

Go to **Device > Sound > Equalizer**. You can save up to 5 custom presets per device. Presets are stored on the device itself, so they also work when the device is connected to another phone.

## Find My Lumora

Find My Lumora shows the last location where your device was connected to the app. For earbuds you can also play a sound on each earbud separately (only when they are in Bluetooth range and out of the case).

## Multipoint

Pulse Pro, Halo ANC and Aria X2 can connect to two devices at the same time. Enable it under **Device > Connections > Multipoint**. Pulse, Halo Lite and Aria Mini do not support multipoint.

## Troubleshooting the app

- **The app does not find my device:** turn Bluetooth off and on, then make sure the device is not connected to another phone.
- **The app shows "Device not registered":** the device is registered to another Lumora account. The previous owner must remove it under **Settings > My devices**, or contact support with a proof of purchase.
- **Battery percentage looks wrong:** fully charge the device once, then fully discharge it, to recalibrate the battery indicator.
