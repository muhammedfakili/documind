# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders and payment

**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. In the EU we also accept iDEAL and Klarna (pay in 3 installments, interest-free).

**Can I get an invoice for my company?**
Yes. Enter your company name and VAT number at checkout. The invoice is emailed when the order ships and is also available under **Orders** in your account.

**Do you offer student discounts?**
Yes, 15% off full-priced products for verified students through our partner UNiDAYS. The discount cannot be combined with other promotions.

## Products

**Which Lumora earbuds have active noise cancellation?**
Pulse Pro has active noise cancellation (ANC) with a transparency mode. The standard Pulse does not have ANC.

**Can I use one earbud on its own?**
Yes, both Pulse and Pulse Pro support single-earbud mode for either side.

**Are the earbuds compatible with iPhone and Android?**
Yes. All Lumora products use standard Bluetooth and work with iOS 16+, Android 11+, Windows 10+ and macOS 12+. Some features (custom EQ, firmware updates) need the Lumora app.

**Is Halo Lite still available?**
Halo Lite was discontinued in March 2026. It is still covered by warranty and supported with firmware updates until March 2028. Existing stock may still be sold as Lumora Certified Renewed.

## Warranty and repairs

**How long is the warranty?**
It depends on the product and the country of purchase. See the warranty terms and the warranty matrix for the exact period.

**Do I need my receipt for a warranty claim?**
If you bought directly from Lumora, no: we find the order from your serial number. For purchases from retailers, a proof of purchase showing the date is required.

## Account and app

**I forgot my Lumora app password.**
Tap **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes.

**Can I delete my account?**
Yes, in the app go to **Settings > Account > Delete account**. Deletion is permanent after 14 days.
