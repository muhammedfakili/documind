# Lumora Pulse — Firmware Release Notes

## 3.2.1 — August 27, 2026
- Fixed: error E-214 could appear too early after charging in warm environments. The battery temperature threshold is now 45 °C.
- Improved: faster reconnection when taking the earbuds out of the case (average 1.1 s, previously 1.8 s).

## 3.2.0 — June 4, 2026
- New: single-earbud mode now supports touch controls on either side.
- New: low-latency "Game Mode" (triple-tap the left earbud).
- Fixed: case battery percentage in the app could stay at 99%.

## 3.1.0 — February 10, 2026
- New: Find My Lumora can play a sound on each earbud separately.
- Improved: call microphone noise reduction in windy conditions.

## 3.0.2 — November 18, 2025
- Fixed: rare audio crackling at maximum volume on some Android phones.
