# Lumora Aria X2 — Firmware Release Notes

## 2.4.0 — September 15, 2026
- New: "Outdoor" EQ preset that boosts low frequencies for open spaces.
- Improved: battery indicator accuracy below 20%.

## 2.3.1 — July 2, 2026
- Fixed: the speaker could stay in pairing mode after a Party Mode session ended.

## 2.3.0 — April 21, 2026
- New: Party Mode supports up to 8 Aria X2 speakers (previously 4).
- New: auto-off timer configurable in the app (15, 30 or 60 minutes, or never).

## 2.2.0 — January 13, 2026
- New: multipoint connection to two devices at the same time.
